How it plays

A game takes an afternoon and a transit pass. Everyone plays from their own phone.

Hide

The hiders get a head start to pick a station and find a spot near it. Then they settle in and answer honestly.

Ask

Seekers spend credits on questions. “Are you within 1 km?” “North or south of me?” The app checks every answer.

Close in

Each answer shades out part of the map. Ride the subway toward what's left and catch them before time runs out.

A real map, a real game

Built on OpenStreetMap, with your city's real stations, parks and rivers.

Questions that cut the map

Every question rules out a different shape, so picking the right one is the game.

PingAre they within a set distance of you?
BearingAre they north or south of you, or east or west?
WarmerWalk a bit. Are you closer than before?
DivideWhich side of a river, avenue or line are they on?

Fair by design

Most answers come straight from the hider's real location, so nobody has to take anyone's word for it. And the seekers never see the hider on the map.

Safe to play

Highways, rail lines and water are off-limits, hosts can add their own zones, and a safety button is always one tap away.
