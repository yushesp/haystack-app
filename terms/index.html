Last updated October 15, 2026

Haystack is a real-world game. You and your friends move through real streets, parks and stations. These terms are the deal between you and us (Haystack, "we") when you use the app. By playing, you agree to them.

Who can play

You must be 18 or older to use Haystack. The app asks you to confirm this when you create your profile or join a game.

You can play as a guest. If you save your account with Sign in with Apple, you can sign in on another phone and keep your history.

You play at your own risk

Haystack happens in the physical world, where there are cars, bikes, trains, water, weather, other people and private property. The app cannot see any of that. You are responsible for your own safety and for how you get around.

To the fullest extent the law allows, you accept the risks of playing, including injury, loss or damage to property, fines and travel costs, and you agree that we are not liable for them. Nothing in these terms limits liability that cannot legally be limited.

Obey the law. Do not trespass, enter restricted or dangerous areas, break traffic rules, or play while driving. Off-limits zones, curfews, the safety briefing and the panic button are there to help, not to replace your judgment.

Off-limits zones come from OpenStreetMap (water, highways and railway lines) and from circles the host draws. Map data can be incomplete, out of date or wrong, and a place that is not marked is not thereby safe or allowed. Stay out of anywhere dangerous or private whether or not the map marks it.

The panic button ends the game for everyone and shows your location to every player in it, so they can come and help. It does not contact emergency services for you; it offers to call them, and you should call them yourself whenever anyone is in danger.

Play fair, be decent

Do not use Haystack to follow, harass or frighten anyone, including people who are not playing. Do not spoof your location to cheat, interfere with the service, or try to read data that is not yours.

Snapshot photos must not show other people without their consent, or anything illegal, explicit, hateful or violent. The same goes for display names.

Haystack is made for playing with friends. Be kind, and keep it fun for everyone.

A host can remove a player from their lobby before the game starts.
