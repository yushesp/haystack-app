Playing safely

Last updated October 15, 2026

The best games of Haystack end with everyone together, swapping stories. Here’s how to make sure yours do.

Before you start

Agree on the play area, an end time and a meeting point with your group. Charge your phone. Tell someone outside the game where you will be.

Uptown is a race across a city. You can walk, run or take the subway, so wear shoes you can walk in all afternoon. Inside Job is played together as a group, at your own pace.

While you play

Never run into traffic, across tracks or into water, and never run for a train. No game is worth it.

Stay out of private property, construction sites, railroad property and anywhere you wouldn’t normally be allowed.

Do not play while driving or cycling. Stop, then look at your phone.

Be kind to the public. They did not sign up for this. If a challenge needs a stranger, ask nicely and take no for an answer.

If a challenge isn’t safe where you are, don’t do it. Pick another one or let it go.

The panic button

In Uptown, the red button on the map ends the game for everyone immediately, shares your last location with the other players in the game and offers to call emergency services. Use it whenever you feel unsafe; nobody will ask why.

In Inside Job your group is together, so just say so and stop. The host can end the game for everyone from the players list.

Getting help

If you are hosting, you can remove anyone from the game, in the lobby or while you play.

You can also write to haystackapp.help@gmail.com. If anyone is in danger, call your local emergency number first.
