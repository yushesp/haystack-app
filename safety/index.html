Playing safely

Last updated October 15, 2026

The best games of Haystack end with everyone together, swapping stories. Here’s how to make sure yours do.

Before you start

Agree on the play area, the curfew and a meeting point with your group. Charge your phone. Tell someone outside the game where you will be.

Pick a scale that suits the group. Block games are on foot and over in an hour.

While you play

Never run into traffic, across tracks or into water. No game is worth it.

Stay out of private property, construction sites, railroad property and anywhere you wouldn’t normally be allowed.

Red hatching on the map is off-limits: water, highways and railway lines from OpenStreetMap, and any circles the host added. Hosts, mark anything else your group should avoid before you start. Nobody can put their Nest inside a zone, but the map cannot know everything, so use your judgment too.

Do not play while driving or cycling. Stop, then look at your phone.

Be kind to the public. They did not sign up for this.

Hide by blending in. Don’t hide anywhere unsafe, locked or out of reach of help.

The panic button

The red button in every game ends the game for everyone immediately, shares your last location with the other players in the game and offers to call emergency services. Use it whenever you feel unsafe; nobody will ask why.

Getting help

If you are hosting, you can remove anyone from your lobby before the game starts.

You can also write to haystackapp.help@gmail.com. If anyone is in danger, call your local emergency number first.
