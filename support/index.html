Support

Something not working, or a question about the game? Email us at haystackapp.help@gmail.com and we'll get back to you, usually within a couple of days.

It helps to include your phone model, the app version (Settings, at the bottom) and what you were doing when it went wrong.

Common questions

How do friends join my game? Start a game and share the 6-character code. Friends tap Join with a code. Nobody needs an account.

Why does Haystack need my location? Challenges, the map and the finish line depend on where you are. Location is only used while a game is running. See the Privacy Policy.

Where can I play Uptown? In New York City and San Francisco. Inside Job plays anywhere.

How do I delete my account? Settings → Delete account. It takes effect right away.

Someone's in danger. Call your local emergency number first. In a game, press and hold the panic button to end it and show your group where you are.
